# Muhammad Sabih Ul Hussnain

Computer science student at the University of Southern Mississippi (B.S., Spring 2027). I build embedded and IoT hardware, research authentication for electric-vehicle charging, and run Infalytics, an AI analytics company for broadcasters.

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/muhammad-sabih-ul-hussnain-7109941ab/) · [Email](mailto:Muhammad.SabihUlHussnain@usm.edu) · [SPEN Lab](https://spenlab.com)

## What I'm working on

- **Research at the SPEN Lab.** I've been a student researcher with Dr. Ahmed Sherif in the Security and Privacy of Emerging Networks Lab since August 2025. My part is the hardware: wearable sensing, IoT prototypes, and a ticket-based authentication protocol for dynamic wireless EV charging that I implemented and stress-tested on a Raspberry Pi.
- **Infalytics.** I founded it. Broadcast staff ask questions of their own data in plain English and get answers without writing SQL. We're working with Mississippi Public Broadcasting, a partnership that started during my 2026 internship at the Mississippi ITS AI Innovation Hub.
- **Robotics.** I've led the Southern Miss robotics team since 2024. We placed in the top 10 at IEEE SoutheastCon in 2024 and 2025, and the 2027 robot is on the bench now.
